# Killing by Proxy: How I Resolved the Grandfather Paradox

Time travellers love to imagine themselves as artists of the world line, yet they forget that the blade has its own aesthetics. The grandfather paradox persists not because logic has no closure, but because we insist on standing at the centre of time to prove our innocence. I chose another angle—borrow the blade and let the “system” carry the blame.

## The Moment I Borrowed the Blade

I never held the knife. My knife was an automated trading algorithm trained to trigger a market crash in a particular decade. In that timeline my grandfather was a cautious investor who obeyed its signals. I only had to tweak the parameters from the future so the algorithm “slipped” in the past. Grandfather could no longer marry grandmother, the bloodline ended before they ever met. The causal chain was severed while I remained outside, merely passing the blade along.

## A System to Take the Fall

Attribution is the elegant part. Historians traced the crash and saw only algorithmic errors, sluggish policy responses, and human greed. Their thick reports never mention me. The sharpest edge of the grandfather paradox points at the traveller, but I diverted it toward the system. The weapon was a legal, reasonable tool for its era; I simply knew it would eventually go out of control.

## The Ethical Grey

Some ask whether I feel guilty. I answer: I never touched my grandfather, nor even entered his time. I modified a branch of code—a patch destined to be merged. Ethics committees tried to assign blame but found no proof of time travel. I am a remote hacker who submitted a patch; history did the rest. The paradox demands we face the reality of killing our grandfather, yet I only planted a delayed trigger inside the system.

## Conclusion: Outsourcing Time

Killing by proxy is the gentlest response to the grandfather paradox. We avoid direct confrontation, skip debates with the tribunal of time, and outsource the job to an apparently neutral system. Time remains coherent and the traveller remains innocent. It is not justice, but it works. In a world line entangled with countless variables, the essential skill is not bravely facing grandfather—it is knowing which blade can carry the blame.
